## What it does
Monitoring: jurisdiction watchlists, change summarization, control mapping, gap flags, owner assignment and deadlines.

Key capabilities:
- Continuous monitoring with explainable AI findings and exposure impact
- One-click playbooks routed to your GRC stack
- Weekly compliance digest with regulatory change totals
- Connects to the rest of the Zion App Network for end-to-end compliance operations
